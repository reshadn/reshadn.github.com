Good questions

Built around trust.
And real work.

A clear mandate for the agents, a clear role for your people, and an honest view of the results.

Is this another CRM subscription?

No. The proposed offer is a managed fundraising function, powered by a coordinated team of AI agents and human supervision. Donor records and integrations support the work behind the scenes. What you hire is the team and its execution.

Is the full agent team live today?

This website is a concept preview, not a demonstration of production-ready agents. The interactive examples use fictional data. Integrations, agent capabilities, permissions, and human support will be verified before any pilot work is agreed or launched.

Will agents contact donors or spend money without us?

No unapproved sending or spending. Your organization sets the approval policy and budget limits. Sensitive messages, solicitations, ad launches, and material changes require the agreed review. The sample controls on this site cannot take external action.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

Not as a starting requirement. Discovery identifies the donor data, donation systems, email tools, and ad accounts needed for the pilot. Compatibility and access must be confirmed. No specific integration is represented here as already live.

How much does the team cost?

Pilot and ongoing pricing are scoped to your donor data volume, integrations, human support, and campaign workload. Advertising spend is funded separately by the nonprofit. You approve the deliverables, team fee, and media cap before work starts.

Do you guarantee a fundraising result?

No. The team is accountable for the agreed work and transparent performance reporting, not a promised dollar return. Reviews distinguish activity, qualified pipeline, unpaid commitments, actual receipts, attribution limits, and operating costs.
